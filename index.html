FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before you apply

It is a long-term multiple-entry tourist visa issued by the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia that remains valid for 10 years, allowing eligible passport holders to visit the country repeatedly for tourism, without needing to reapply for each trip.

Each visit under the 10-year tourist visa typically allows a stay of up to 90 days, and you may enter and exit multiple times during the visa's validity period.

You will generally need a valid passport (6+ months remaining), a recent passport-style photo, proof of travel insurance, and a return or onward ticket. Our team will guide you through the exact checklist for your country.

Final approval is issued by the Saudi authorities. Every package includes a full document review to maximize your chances of approval, and our 10-Year Business Visa package is backed by our dedicated agent support.

Once submitted, you'll receive email updates at every stage (plus WhatsApp updates for the 10-Year Business Visa package), plus a dedicated tracking link so you always know your application status.
